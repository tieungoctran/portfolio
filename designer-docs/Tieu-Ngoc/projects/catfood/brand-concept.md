# Brand Concept: Miu Miu - Thuc An Cho Meo

## Brand Identity

### Ten thuong hieu: **Miu Miu**
- Phat am tu nhien, de nho, gan gui voi nguoi Viet
- "Miu" = tieng keu cua meo trong tieng Viet (onomatopoeia)
- Lap lai tao cam giac dang yeu, playful
- De doc, de viet, de nhan dien tren social media

### Tagline chinh:
**"Ngon tu mieng dau. Yeu tu ngay dau."**

### Tagline phu:
- "Meo khoe. Chu vui."
- "Vi meo cung biet chon dieu tot nhat."
- "Khong chi la bua an. La tinh yeu tren tung hat."

## Target Audience

| Thuoc tinh | Chi tiet |
|------------|----------|
| Do tuoi | 25-40 |
| Gioi tinh | Nu 65%, Nam 35% |

| Hanh vi | Coi meo nhu con, san sang chi tien cho chat luong |

| Insight | "Toi muon cho meo an ngon nhung khong biet chon gi" |

## Brand Voice

- **Giong dieu:** Am ap, hai huoc nhe nhang, hieu biet
- **Khong:** Le me, giao dieu, qua ky thuat
- **Co:** Tro chuyen nhu ban be yeu meo, pha chut wit
- **Cam xuc:** Yeu thuong + tu hao la "sen" cua meo

## Social Media Ad Copy (10 slides)

### Slide 1 - Hero / Brand Introduction
**Headline:** Miu Miu
**Sub:** Ngon tu mieng dau. Yeu tu ngay dau.
**Body:** Thuc an cho meo - Nguyen lieu tu nhien. Dinh duong toan dien.

### Slide 2 - Emotional Hook (PAS Formula)
**Headline:** Sen oi, meo co that su thich an khong?
**Body:** Meo ken an khong phai vi kho tinh. Ma vi chua gap dung mon. Miu Miu - cong thuc khien meo "doi bat" moi ngay.
**CTA:** Miu Miu. Vi meo cung biet chon.

### Slide 3 - Ingredient Focus
**Headline:** 100% thit ga ta tuoi
**Sub:** Khong bot. Khong chat bao quan. Khong lua doi.
**Body:** Miu Miu chi dung nguyen lieu ma ban co the doc ten - va meo co the cam nhan.

### Slide 4 - Lifestyle / Bonding
**Headline:** Bua an cua meo. Hanh phuc cua ban.
**Body:** Moi lan meo an ngon, la mot lan ban biet minh da chon dung. Miu Miu - khong chi la thuc an, la tinh yeu tren tung hat.

### Slide 5 - Product Line (Flavors)
**Headline:** 4 vi. 1 tinh yeu.
**Body:**
- Ca ngu Dai Duong
- Ga ta & Cam thao
- Ca hoi Na Uy
- Thit bo Uc thao moc
**CTA:** Meo ban thich vi nao?

### Slide 6 - Social Proof / Trust
**Headline:** 12,847 "sen" da chon Miu Miu
**Body:** "Tu ngay doi sang Miu Miu, Long muot hon, mat sang hon. Va quan trong nhat - het ken an!" - Chi Linh, TP.HCM
**Badge:** Duoc thu y khuyen dung

### Slide 7 - Nutrition Facts (Educational)
**Headline:** Meo can gi? Miu Miu co du.
**Body:**
- Protein 38% - co bap chac khoe
- Omega-3 & 6 - long muot bong
- Taurine - mat sang, tim khoe
- Khong ngu coc - de tieu hoa
**Sub:** Cong thuc duoc phat trien boi chuyen gia dinh duong thu y.

### Slide 8 - Playful / Humor
**Headline:** Meo nha ban co dang "ghet" ban khong?
**Body:** Dau hieu: quay mat khi thay bat an, khong them nhin, di cho khac. Giai phap: doi mon. Miu Miu - meo ghien tu lan dau.

### Slide 9 - Premium Positioning
**Headline:** Khong phai thuc an re nhat. La thuc an dung nhat.
**Body:** Miu Miu khong canh tranh ve gia. Chung toi canh tranh ve chat luong nguyen lieu, tieu chuan san xuat, va su hai long cua meo ban.

### Slide 10 - CTA / Closing
**Headline:** Thu Miu Miu hom nay.
**Sub:** Meo khoe. Chu vui.
**Body:** Mien phi van chuyen don hang dau tien. Cam ket hoan tien 100% neu meo khong thich.
**CTA:** Dat hang ngay tai miumiu.vn
