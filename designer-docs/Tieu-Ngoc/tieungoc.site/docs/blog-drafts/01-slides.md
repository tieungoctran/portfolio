# 3 Nut That Co Chai Trong Quy Trinh

- Tim y tuong: 30 den 60 phut moi batch
- Tao mockup va chinh sua: 15 den 25 phut moi ban
- Xuat file va kiem tra chat luong: 5 den 10 phut moi ban
- Tong cong: khoang 40 phut cho 1 ban hoan chinh
- Thuc te sau meeting va revision: chi duoc 5 den 8 ban moi ngay

^ Day la 3 nut that co chai. Tim y tuong mat 30 den 60 phut moi batch. Tao mockup va chinh sua chi tiet mat 15 den 25 phut moi ban. Xuat file dung spec va kiem tra chat luong mat them 5 den 10 phut. Tong cong khoang 40 phut cho mot ban hoan chinh. Trong 8 tieng lam viec, toi da chi lam duoc 12 ban. Sau khi tru meeting, email va revision, thuc te chi duoc 5 den 8 ban.

# Giai Phap: 3 Tang Cua AI-First Workflow

- Tang 1: AI Generative cho Ideation
- Tang 2: Photoshop Scripting cho Execution
- Tang 3: Kiem Tra Chat Luong Tu Dong

^ Giai phap cua toi la quy trinh AI-First Workflow 3 tang. Moi tang giai quyet mot nut that co chai cu the. Tang 1 dung cong cu AI generative de tim y tuong. Tang 2 dung Photoshop Scripting va Actions de tu dong hoa thiet ke. Tang 3 tu dong hoa viec kiem tra chat luong. Toi se trinh bay chi tiet tung tang.

# Tang 1: AI Generative Cho Tim Y Tuong

- Midjourney: 1 prompt = 4 phien ban trong 60 giay
- Adobe Firefly Generative Fill: chinh sua truc tiep trong Photoshop
- Banana Pro: nang chat luong hinh anh len chuan in 300 DPI
- Ket qua: Giam tu 30 den 60 phut xuong 10 den 15 phut moi batch

^ Voi tang 1, thay vi ngoi brainstorm tu con so 0, toi dung cong cu AI generative de tao nguyen lieu tho nhanh chong. Midjourney tao concept art voi 4 phien ban moi prompt chi trong 60 giay. Trong 10 phut toi co 40 concept de chon loc. Adobe Firefly cho phep chinh sua truc tiep trong Photoshop. Va Banana Pro nang chat luong hinh anh len chuan in an 300 DPI. Ket qua la thoi gian tim y tuong giam tu 30 den 60 phut xuong chi con 10 den 15 phut cho mot batch.

---

# Tang 2: Photoshop Scripting Va Actions

- Batch Mockup Generator: 20 den 30 mockup trong 5 phut
- Color Variant Actions: 5 den 8 mau tu 1 design goc
- Auto-Export Script: ten file chuan, DPI dung, color profile dung
- Ket qua: Giam tu 15 den 25 phut xuong 2 den 3 phut moi ban

^ Tang 2 la noi tao ra khac biet lon nhat. Thay vi lam tung ban thu cong, toi viet Photoshop Script de tu dong hoa cac buoc lap lai. Batch Mockup Generator nhan folder design va template, tu dong dat vi tri, thay doi kich thuoc va chinh perspective. Mot lan chay ra 20 den 30 mockup trong 5 phut. Actions cho Color Variants tu dong tao 5 den 8 phien ban mau tu mot design goc. Va Auto-Export Script tu dong xuat file theo quy tac dat ten chuan, dung DPI va color profile. Ket qua la thoi gian thiet ke giam tu 15 den 25 phut xuong chi con 2 den 3 phut moi ban.

---

# Tang 3: Kiem Tra Chat Luong Tu Dong

- Pre-flight Check Script: do phan giai, che do mau, kich thuoc file
- Naming Convention Validator: chuan format cho tung nen tang
- Visual Spot Check: mat nguoi chi can kiem 10 den 15 phan tram mau

^ Khi san luong tang gap 3 lan, viec kiem tra chat luong phai theo kip. Khong the kiem tung file bang mat. Pre-flight Check Script tu dong xac minh do phan giai, che do mau va kich thuoc file truoc khi upload. Naming Convention Validator dam bao ten file theo dung format cua tung nen tang. Va Visual Spot Check van can mat nguoi, nhung chi cho 10 den 15 phan tram mau. 85 phan tram con lai da duoc script kiem tra.

# 3 Sai Lam Toi Da Mac

- Sai lam 1: Dung AI thay the hoan toan. Output nhin generic, thieu ca tinh thuong hieu. AI la don bay, khong phai thay the.
- Sai lam 2: Tu dong hoa truoc khi chuan hoa. Script chay duoc nhung output khong nhat quan. SOP truoc, script sau.
- Sai lam 3: Khong do luong. Cam thay nhanh hon nhung khong co so lieu. Theo doi SKU moi ngay, thoi gian xoay vong, ty le sua lai tu ngay dau.

^ Toi da mac 3 sai lam lon. Thu nhat, toi co dung AI de thay the hoan toan quy trinh thiet ke. Ket qua la san pham nhin generic, thieu ca tinh thuong hieu. Bai hoc la AI la don bay, khong phai thay the. Con mat tham my va hieu biet thuong hieu van la cua designer. Thu hai, toi tu dong hoa truoc khi chuan hoa quy trinh. Script chay duoc nhung output khong nhat quan. Bai hoc la phai chuan hoa quy trinh thu cong truoc, roi moi tu dong hoa. SOP truoc, script sau. Thu ba, toi khong do luong. Hai thang dau chi cam thay nhanh hon ma khong co so lieu cu the. Khong thuyet phuc duoc team. Bai hoc la phai theo doi cac chi so tu ngay dau tien.

# Ke Hoach Hanh Dong 4 Tuan

- Tuan 1: Cai Adobe Firefly trong Photoshop 2024. Lam quen Generative Fill
- Tuan 2: Hoc Photoshop Actions co ban. Ghi lai 1 action cho viec lap lai nhieu nhat
- Tuan 3: Thu Midjourney cho tim y tuong. Bat dau voi niche quen thuoc
- Tuan 4: Viet script dau tien cho batch export. Don gian nhat, hieu qua nhat
- Neu chi chon 1 tang: chon Tang 2 Photoshop Scripting cho ROI cao nhat

^ Day la ke hoach hanh dong 4 tuan. Tuan 1, cai Adobe Firefly da tich hop san trong Photoshop phien ban 2024 tro len. Dung Generative Fill mot tuan de lam quen. Tuan 2, hoc Photoshop Actions co ban. Ghi lai mot action cho viec ban lam lap lai nhieu nhat, vi du resize va export. Tuan 3, thu Midjourney de tim y tuong. Bat dau voi niche ban da quen de so sanh chat luong. Tuan 4, viet script dau tien cho batch export, day la buoc don gian nhat nhung hieu qua nhat. Neu ban chi co the chon mot tang, hay chon Tang 2 Photoshop Scripting vi no cho ROI cao nhat.
